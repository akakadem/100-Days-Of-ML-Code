## 01. AI Lead Qualification System

**Category:** Sales & Revenue

### What it is

Scores and ranks inbound leads using CRM data, firmographics, intent signals, and conversation history so sales only spends time on high-probability opportunities.

### Step-by-step process

1. Connect lead sources (web forms, ads, chat, imports) and CRM fields.
2. Define Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) criteria and disqualification rules.
3. Configure scoring from historical won/lost deals and intent signals.
4. Auto-enrich leads (company size, industry, tech stack, funding).
5. Score and tier leads (A/B/C) with explainable reasons.
6. Route A-tier leads to reps instantly; nurture lower tiers.
7. Log outcomes and retrain scoring thresholds monthly.

### 3 applicable examples

1. AGSE Tooling website demo requests scored and routed to enterprise vs SMB reps.
2. Trade-show badge scans enriched overnight and prioritized for next-day follow-up.
3. Inbound partner referrals auto-qualified against ICP before SDR outreach.

### Advantages / benefits

- Removes bias and inconsistency from manual triage.
- Creates a measurable, auditable qualification standard.
- Frees SDRs from low-value research work.

### Monetization benefits

Higher close rates and shorter sales cycles increase revenue per rep; sell as a managed AGSE lead-ops module or usage-based enrichment add-on.

### Faster · Better · Cheaper

- **Faster:** Qualification in seconds instead of hours of research.
- **Better:** Consistent ICP fit scoring with transparent reasons.
- **Cheaper:** Fewer wasted discovery calls and lower cost-per-opportunity.
